CHAPTER 1: The Fundamental Unit of Life (Cell)

Historical Timeline

  • Robert Hooke (1665): Observed cork cells; coined "Cell".
  • Anton van Leeuwenhoek (1674): First observed living cells.
  • Robert Brown (1831): Discovered the Nucleus.
  • Schleiden & Schwann (1838-39): Formulated Cell Theory.
  • Rudolf Virchow (1855): "Omnis cellula-e cellula".

Plasma Membrane & Transport

Fluid Mosaic Model (Singer & Nicolson, 1972). Features diffusion, osmosis, and endocytosis mechanisms.
